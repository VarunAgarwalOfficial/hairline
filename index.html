Hairline figures

Six isometric line drawings that move with the pointer. Built with the Hairline engine. Source.

  • bloomShrubs round a house grow where the pointer waters the garden.
  • flipA clamshell phone whose lid swings open and shut as the pointer moves across.
  • snapA flip phone that opens on hover; its buttons can be pressed by click or keyboard.
  • storeysA two-storey house that opens into layers as the pointer moves up.
  • swingsA swing set with four kids; the swing under the pointer is pushed and its neighbours follow.
  • two-swingsTwo kids on swings; hovering pushes one, and the other follows.
